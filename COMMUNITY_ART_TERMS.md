# Community Art Terms

Last updated: October 17, 2026

These terms apply when you submit artwork to the ENGENEverse Community Board.

## Your art and permission

You keep ownership of artwork you create. You may submit only work you made or have permission to share. Do not submit someone else’s art, private images, or material that you do not have rights to use. Fan art may include third-party characters or people; you are responsible for respecting the rights that apply to those materials.

By submitting, you give the ENGENEverse site operator a non-exclusive, worldwide, no-fee license to store, review, display, and share your submission on the Community Board and through its share links. This limited license lasts only while the submission is on the Board. It does not transfer ownership, allow merchandise or commercial exploitation, or let the operator claim authorship. You can ask the site operator to remove your submission; the license ends when it is removed, except for copies already shared by other people or temporary technical backups.

Your chosen artist name or pseudonym is displayed with the approved artwork and included in its share text. Do not enter an email address or other private information as your credit name.

## Review and removal

Submissions stay private until approved by the site creator. Submissions may be screened automatically and reviewed by a person. A safety screen can make mistakes and is not a guarantee that every harmful or infringing image will be detected. The creator may reject or remove content that is unsafe, unrelated, infringing, or otherwise unsuitable for the Board. Users may report published artwork for review.

## Sharing

Approved artwork can be shared by a direct link to its public image. Other people may save or reshare an image after it is shared. Removing the Board post cannot recall copies already made by others.

## Contact

For removal or copyright concerns, contact the site creator through the Community Board.

This is a plain-language community policy, not a substitute for legal advice. Applicable law may provide additional rights or obligations.
